## Being straight about it

- **1f8d6 is where the work went.** It had 11 notes running 4.4 s past the last onset — that is
  literally what you heard. Now 1, and its end-of-song accuracy roughly halves its error.
- **1f767 barely moved.** Precision +0.003, drift unchanged, and its diagonal share got *worse*
  (0.449 → 0.490). If this one still feels off at the end, that is real and the lever did not
  address it.
- **1f913 got very slightly worse on precision** (0.933 → 0.923) while its diagonals improved a lot
  (0.542 → 0.412). It was already your cleanest song, so it had least to gain.
- **Diagonals are still well above human** everywhere (0.41–0.49 vs 0.354). Your "for-sport
  diagonals" complaint is *not* fixed, and on a clean 200-map Expert cohort it is worse than I first
  wrote: human diagonal share **falls** steadily as passages get faster (0.355 → 0.346 → 0.301 →
  0.236) while ours **rises** (0.466 → 0.476 → 0.536 → 0.631). In the fastest passages that is
  **2.7×** the human rate, exactly where diagonals punish. That is the next thing to build.
- **Half the supporting evidence is circular** and I would rather say so than let it stand: the
  onset-evidence lever is scored partly against the same onset detector it consumes. The independent
  measurement is the rhythm axis, which improved by a small but real amount.

## The one question that matters

Does the ending of a song now land properly — and does anything about the AFTER maps feel *worse*
than the BEFORE ones? The suite says the trade is free; it has said that wrongly twice.
