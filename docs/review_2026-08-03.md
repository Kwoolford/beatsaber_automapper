## Things I'd rather you knew before playing

- **The onset-evidence lever made reachability WORSE** — 1f333 0.098 → 0.157, 1f913 0.137 → 0.194.
  It concentrates notes into dense windows, which manufactures far-and-soon transitions. I only found
  this because your correction made me build the metric. AFTER2 more than repairs it, but if AFTER2
  still feels awkward somewhere, that interaction is the first place I would look.
- **Reach distances did not shrink** (median 2.83 throughout, matching human). Only the *rushed* ones
  were eased. That was deliberate: humans reach **further** than we do (p90 3.61 vs our 3.16) and
  simply give the movement time. A version that "fixed" this by making everything small would be
  worse — the stronger setting I tested did exactly that, so I rejected it.
- **Peak density is what I am most suspicious of.** 1f333 peaks at 9.5 notes/sec against a human
  median of 5.5 (inside the human p90 of 12, but well above typical). If the busiest moments feel
  like too much, that is real and it is `ONSET_EVIDENCE` overreaching.
- **1f8d6 still drifts** (0.253 vs human −0.006) even though its post-music notes are gone. Its
  *ending* is fixed; its last-fifth accuracy is not.
